Pay every small supplier before the legal deadline.

Indian law gives buyers 15 days to pay a micro or small supplier, or up to 45 days with a written agreement. Pay late and you lose the tax deduction for that year. Claude reads the invoice. Clear45 works out the deadline with fixed rules and keeps it live for your finance team.

  1. Ask Claude. Drop an invoice into Claude and say “log this bill in Clear45”.
  2. See it here. The bill appears with its legal due date and a red, amber or green countdown.
  3. Edit and approve. Fix any field, approve, mark it paid and download the compliance register.
